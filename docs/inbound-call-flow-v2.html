01What changed from v1.0

Two routing corrections drive this revision. Both are drawn below before anything else, because a builder who gets only these two right has fixed the errors that mattered.

#Areav1.0v2.0
1Realtor / agent routingRealtor → MariaelenaRealtor → GEN (X2 → T9 / T10)
2SabelliTreated as somebody callers could be sent toNotification and review recipient only, on approved T22 cases. No transfer node exists.
3Legal / agency / mailerMixed into normal qualificationIsolated global layer G-LEGAL / G-MAILER → T22 → Juan + Sabelli
4DNCHandled ad hocGlobal node G-DNC → T21, sets dnc_requested = true
5EndingsSome branches ran out without a disposition24 named endings, every branch terminates
6Build ownerBuild: SethBuild / Retell implementation: [CURRENT BUILDER]

Retired ending IDs. T7 and T8 were the v1.0 realtor-to-Mariaelena endings. They are retired, which is why the realtor branch now ends at T9 and T10.

Dark

Retell: Logic Split
Green
Successful transfer or completed outcome. The live call moved.
Retell: Transfer Call · Ending
Yellow
Notification and follow-up. Information moves, the call does not.
Retell: Function — webhook
Red
Hard stops — legal, agency, mailer, DNC. These override everything.
Retell: Global node · Ending
Purple

Arrows

  • →Solid arrow — Transfer Call. The audio path moves with it. Only five of these exist in the whole build.
  • ⇢Dashed arrow — Send Notification. A webhook fires, the caller stays with the AI or the call ends. Sabelli is only ever reached this way.

Peninsula Plumbing stays a plumbing call

A plumbing caller is never converted into a Twin Home Buyer seller lead during that call. A5 takes the plumbing message, N11 sends it to the plumbing intake, and the record lands in the plumbing log — not the seller pipeline. Whether a live transfer to a plumbing line is permitted is Pending confirmation; until then plumbing calls are message-only.

What the AI must not do on a mailer or legal call

  • ✗Explain the mailer.
  • ✗Defend the company.
  • ✗Confirm an allegation.
  • ✗Deny an allegation.
  • ✗Speculate, or improvise an answer.
  • ✓Capture the approved minimum, preserve the transcript, send N14, end at T22.

The Sabelli rule, stated once more

N14 is a notification. The call does not move. There is no Transfer to Sabelli node in this build and none may be added. Sabelli appears exactly once in the entire flow — as a review recipient on T22.

Proposed hard-stop precedence, Pending Juan confirmation: G-DNC → G-LEGAL → G-MAILER → G-HUMAN → normal flow. If a caller both requests DNC and raises a legal or mailer matter, the proposal is to honour the DNC at T21 and send the T22 notification, recording both endings. Do not build the combination rule until it is confirmed.
